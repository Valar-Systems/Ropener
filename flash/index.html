⚙️ Flash your Ropener

Plug the device into this computer over USB, then click Install. The installer auto-detects your board by its chip and flashes the matching firmware:

  • VAL3000 — ESP32-C3
  • VAL3100 — ESP32-C6
⚠️ Your browser can't flash — use desktop Chrome or Edge. ⚠️ This page must be served over HTTPS (or localhost) to flash.

After flashing, the installer can also connect the device to your Wi-Fi. Need help? See the Firmware Guide.
